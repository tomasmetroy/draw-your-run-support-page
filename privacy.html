Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Draw Your Run does not require an account and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Location

The app requests your device's location, with your permission, only to center the map near you when you open the app. Your location is never stored, transmitted, or shared with anyone.

Drawn Routes

When you draw a route on the map, the coordinates of that drawn route (not your real device location) are sent to a third-party elevation lookup service (Open-Meteo) solely to estimate how much climbing the route involves. This data is not linked to your identity, is not stored by us, and is not used for advertising or tracking.

No Accounts, No Ads, No Analytics

Draw Your Run does not collect any other personal data, does not display ads, and does not use analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind.
